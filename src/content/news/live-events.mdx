**Shenzhen, China, Sep. 2, 2024** – CNDLive is proud to announce the **C6**, a compact H.265 video encoder featuring dual interface 4K HDMI and 3G-SDI input and loop. Compatible with a wide range of audio and video capture equipment, the C6 is designed to be versatile and user-friendly for creators and professionals alike.

## Designed for Usability

The C6 Encoder is engineered with user-friendliness in mind. It features an intuitive setup menu that provides easy access to advanced configurations and system management. 

Its robust connection management ensures a stable and reliable network connection, while advanced user management enhances system security. With customizable time and timezone settings, the C6 Encoder caters to global live streaming needs. Additionally, the one-click upgrade and restart functionality ensures that your device always performs at its best.

## Key Features

### 1. HDMI 2.0 and 3G-SDI Inputs
The C6 supports **HDMI 2.0 with up to 3840x2160p30** video input and loop, along with 3G-SDI input and loop. You can mix HDMI and SDI sources, supporting up to 1920x1080p30, making it suitable for applications such as education, training, and professional broadcasting.

### 2. Multistreaming Capabilities
The C6 supports a wide array of protocols, including **NDI|HX2, NDI|HX3, SRT, RTMP, RTMPS, HLS, and TS over UDP**. This enables simultaneous streaming to eight different platforms via main and sub streams, ensuring your content reaches every audience.

### 3. Built-in 2″ LCD Screen
Stay in control with the built-in 2″ LCD screen. It allows you to easily start and stop streaming and recording. The JPEG preview ensures continuous source input monitoring, while real-time insights on system status, device details, network information, recording status, and video input formats are all clearly displayed.

### 4. Advanced Connectivity and Management
The C6 includes **Wi-Fi 6**, SNMP, and remote management capabilities. It also features text/image overlay, an intuitive web UI, and a physical shortcut key. The device offers low latency and reliable quality to ensure an optimal user experience.
